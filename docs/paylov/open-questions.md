# Open Paylov questions

Unresolved Paylov behavior. IDs are stable and never reused. Grades follow the
[reference index](README.md#evidence). When Paylov answers or live evidence settles a question,
record the fact on its owning page and remove the row and every `[OPEN Qn]` marker for it, through
the [sync-paylov-reference skill](../../.agents/skills/sync-paylov-reference/SKILL.md). A maintainer
decision (M) settles a question the same way only when the SDK must act on an answer Paylov does not
give; the owning page then states the assumed answer with its M grade.
